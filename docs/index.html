FAQ

Short answers to the usual questions.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. All games, relax sounds, articles and travel tools work in airplane mode. When you are online, the app can optionally download new articles and current exchange rates, and you can limit that to Wi-Fi or switch it off in Settings.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything described on this page is free, with no ads and no account. Optional in-app purchases (FlightMode Plus and tips) only add extras.

What is FlightMode Plus?

An optional one-time purchase that supports development. Today it unlocks two extra themes (Midnight and Sunset) and a profile badge. Nothing that is free is ever moved behind Plus.

Which languages are supported?

12: English, Czech, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. Some travel articles are available in fewer languages and are shown in English otherwise.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. FlightMode is available for Android only for now.

What data does the app collect?

Your flights, scores and settings stay on your device. Optional anonymous usage statistics can be switched off in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
