# Privacy Policy — FlightMode

**Last updated:** 28 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the FlightMode mobile application ("App"), developed by an individual developer ("we", "us", "our"), handles data. FlightMode is an offline-first companion for flights: it works without an internet connection, and it has no account, login or user profile.

## 2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is **not** sent to us or to any third party:

- **Settings** — language, theme, home currency, sync and analytics preferences, onboarding status
- **Flight information you enter** — departure date/time, duration, optional destination and optional flight number, and your saved flight history ("Flight Passport")
- **Travel checklist** — ticked items and any custom items you add
- **Game progress** — scores, streaks, level stars, achievements
- **Player names** you optionally type for pass-and-play games
- **Cached content** — downloaded articles, article images and exchange rates, so they work offline
- **Purchase status** — whether FlightMode Plus is unlocked (cached so it works offline)

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

## 3. Data That Leaves Your Device

### 3.1 Anonymous product analytics (PostHog) — optional

If analytics is enabled in the app build, the App sends anonymous usage events to **PostHog** (posthog.com), an analytics provider, to help us understand which features are used.

- **What is sent:** general usage events such as app opened, onboarding completed, flight added or edited (only coarse ranges such as "departs in 1–7 days" or "flight 2–5 h", never the exact time), game started or finished and score, article opened or finished, relax exercise and ambient sound used, travel tools opened (checklist, phrasebook, converter), content and exchange-rate sync succeeded or failed, achievements unlocked, and technical error information (error type, a shortened error code with web addresses, file paths and other details removed, and function names from the error location).
- **Identifiers:** a random, anonymous device ID generated by the analytics SDK and stored on your device. It is not linked to your name, e-mail, phone number, advertising ID or any account. Person profiles are disabled.
- **IP address:** like any internet service, PostHog receives your IP address when events are uploaded. The App asks PostHog not to use it for location lookup (GeoIP is disabled).
- **Never sent:** your name, e-mail, phone number, exact location, flight number, exact departure time, player names, checklist text or any other text you type.
- **When it is sent:** events are kept on the device while you are offline and uploaded later, only on networks allowed by your **Settings → Online Sync** choice (Wi-Fi only / Wi-Fi + mobile data / Off).
- **Opting out:** you can switch **Settings → Privacy → Anonymous Analytics** off at any time. The App then stops collecting events and deletes events that have not been uploaded yet. Analytics stays off from the next app start before any event is sent.

### 3.2 Article updates (GitHub)

To deliver new travel articles without an app update, the App periodically downloads a public file (`content/feed.json`) from **raw.githubusercontent.com** (GitHub). Article images are downloaded from the image hosts listed in that file (currently images.unsplash.com). These are plain downloads: no personal data is sent, but the servers receive your IP address and standard request information. This respects your **Online Sync** setting and can be switched off entirely.

### 3.3 Exchange rates (open.er-api.com)

When you are online, the currency converter downloads current exchange rates from **open.er-api.com** (ExchangeRate-API). No personal data is sent; the server receives your IP address and standard request information. This also respects your **Online Sync** setting. Without it, the converter uses rates bundled with the App.

### 3.4 Purchases and tips (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (FlightMode Plus, tips) are processed entirely by **Google Play**. We do not receive or store your payment details. The App only stores locally whether Plus is unlocked. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

### 3.5 Achievements (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games on your device, achievements you unlock in the App are reported to **Google Play Games Services** under your Play Games profile. Google's privacy policy applies. We do not receive your Google account details.

### 3.6 E-mails you choose to send

"Report a Bug" and "Suggest a Feature" open your own e-mail app with our address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the e-mail yourself.

## 4. Device Features and Permissions

- **Notifications** — optional local reminders (e.g. flight-ready, jet-lag sleep window). They are scheduled on the device; no notification data is sent to us.
- **Motion sensors** — used only while you play motion-controlled games (e.g. Tilt Balance, Box Breaker, Maze Muncher tilt mode). Sensor data is processed on the device and never recorded or sent.
- **Foreground service / media playback / audio settings** — used solely so relaxation sounds can keep playing in the background.
- **Internet** — used only for the optional downloads and analytics described above. All core features work offline.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions, and it blocks the advertising ID permission.

## 5. No Selling, No Ads, No Account

We do not sell or rent data. The App contains no advertising SDKs. The App has no user accounts.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The analytics described above are anonymous and can be switched off.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- Local data stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it.
- Anonymous analytics data is retained by PostHog according to the developer's PostHog project settings. Because it is not linked to your identity, we generally cannot single out your events; if you have a request, contact us with as much detail as possible.

## 8. Security

We minimise what leaves the device (see section 3), and all network requests use HTTPS. The security of locally stored data also depends on your device's own security settings.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
